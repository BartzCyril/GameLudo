Gameludo lets you play **Ludo** for free in your browser, against three bots, with no sign-up and no download.

## The project

Gameludo is an independent project, created and maintained by {editorName}, a web developer based in France. The goal is simple: enjoy the classic board game in a few seconds, on a computer or a phone.

## What you will find on the site

- **The game**, with the classic rules: a 6 to bring a pawn out, captures that send pawns back to base, and an exact roll to enter the home column. See the [rules for starting a game](@start).
- **Automatic saving**: close the tab, come back later, and your game will be waiting for you.
- **A complete guide**: the rules, [strategies to win](@offensive) and the [history of the game](@history), from Indian pachisi onwards.
- **Eleven languages**: French, English, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Arabic.

## Free thanks to advertising

The game is and will remain free. It is funded by Google ads, placed away from the board so that they never get in the way of your game. You choose which cookies you accept: see the [privacy policy](@privacy).
